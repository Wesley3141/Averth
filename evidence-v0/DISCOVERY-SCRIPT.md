# Discovery outreach script — separate from any prototype invitation

DO NOT SEND without Wesley's explicit tap naming the recipient,
the channel, and this exact script. This is a discovery
conversation only. It is NOT an invitation to test a prototype,
because no verified demo package exists and no repair has been
validated. Do not combine this script with a technical
contribution, a PR, or an issue post.

## The only question this script asks

Would a pre-submission evidence report have saved you meaningful
review time on a specific past contribution, and would you review
a retrospective analysis of that public case?

## Script (adapt only the bracketed first line, from a real public
signal; never invent a signal)

> Hi [Name], I saw [specific public problem, e.g. your #2243 note
> about AI-assisted contributions costing review time, and the
> #2232 submission that took review time before its gaps became
> clear]. I am working on a small local tool that, for a few
> human-written checks, reports what the record actually shows —
> verified, failed, not observed, or unverifiable — with the
> command, exit code, and timing for each check, instead of
> trusting a summary that says done.
>
> I am not offering a product, a repair, or a measured result.
> The tool is a local prototype under evaluation, it has only
> been run on a toy fixture and public traces, and it has not
> been validated on a real workflow.
>
> Two questions, and a no is a useful answer: first, on that past
> case, would a short report separating what was actually
> verified from what remained unknown have saved you meaningful
> review time, or would it just have been another document to
> read? Second, would you be willing to look at a retrospective
> analysis of that public case only, and correct our
> interpretation where it is wrong?
>
> No installation, no private traces, no repo access, and at most
> one respectful follow-up after 5-7 days, then stop, unless you
> ask for more.
>
> Wesley

## Hard exclusions in every use of this script

- NO repair promise, NO human-approved repair offer.
- NO measured before/after result, NO demo result, NO cost saving.
- NO subscription, NO price, NO paid pilot offer.
- NO sponsorship, NO compute, NO tool-access offer.
- NO Qwen support claim, NO support for any specific model setup
  that has not been validated.
- NO private-trace, private-repo, or installation ask.
- NO claim that the V0 is validated on a real workflow, used by
  any customer, or proven to save time.
- If the person declines, or does not reply, stop. One respectful
  follow-up after 5–7 days at most, then stop. Never re-pitch the
  same person with a prototype invitation unless a verified demo
  package exists and Wesley separately taps that invitation.

## What a yes means, and what it does not mean

A yes means: a discovery conversation, and possibly a
retrospective analysis of a public case, with corrections. It
does not mean a pilot, a user, a buyer, willingness to pay, or
validation of the product. Record the exact problem, the exact
past incident, the workaround they use now, and what result
would justify a later pilot conversation — and nothing more.
